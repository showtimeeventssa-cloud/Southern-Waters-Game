How to play

Move: drag the left joystick. Look: drag the open right side of the screen. The world is rendered in real-time 3D.

Cast: tap Cast to send the float into the water. Wait for a bite, then tap Reel In repeatedly to fight the fish. Keep an eye on line tension; stop reeling briefly if it becomes critical.

Boat: toggle the boat mode to move out from shore. This prototype has basic boat movement and a first-person fishing loop; not all planned equipment or species are implemented yet.
