SOUTHERN WATERS — M8 LANDSCAPE MENU
- Main menu artwork embedded directly in index.html.
- The game menu is presented in a centered 16:9 landscape viewport with transparent menu/water touch targets.
- In portrait orientation, the menu is intentionally hidden and a rotate-to-play screen is shown.
- The web app cannot reliably force iPhone Safari to rotate; turn the phone sideways. For best results open the hosted HTTPS page in Safari.
- This is the menu screen prototype; the artwork's featured waters are menu selections, not surveyed geographic maps, and menu targets show preview feedback rather than launching a full 3D world.
